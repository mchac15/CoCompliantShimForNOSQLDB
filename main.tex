\section*{System Model}

\paragraph{Failure Model.}
Crash recovery is out of scope. The shim is assumed not to crash: in particular not between voting YES and the coordinator's decision. Nothing is persisted (\texttt{locks\_map}, \texttt{transactions\_map} and the write buffers are volatile), so 2PC atomicity holds only under this no-crash assumption. No Byzantine failures. Network is asynchronous: messages may be delayed, lost, duplicated, reordered, not corrupted; coordinator messages are therefore idempotent at the shim. Partitions eventually heal.

\paragraph{Storage Assumptions.}
The underlying NoSQL store is a black box offering only \texttt{read} and \texttt{write}: \texttt{write} is a blind put and no atomicity across keys is assumed. Store operations are blocking and are retried until they succeed, so the protocol has no error path for store failures.

\paragraph{Deadlocks.}
The waits of the execution phase (\Call{lock}{} and \Call{upgrade}{}) are bounded by a timeout: when it expires the subtransaction aborts, which is always safe since it has not executed and voted nothing. This costs liveness only, never CO or 2PC atomicity. The waits in \texttt{prepare} have no timeout: a cross-shim cycle there (T1 before T2 on one shim, T2 before T1 on another) is a 2PC-level deadlock and is left to the coordinator, e.g.\ its vote timeout.

\paragraph{Coordinator Assumptions.}
\texttt{txn\_id}s are assigned by the coordinator. It calls \texttt{start} at most once per \texttt{txn\_id}, sends \texttt{end} at most once, after the transaction's last \texttt{get}/\texttt{put}, and sends no \texttt{get}/\texttt{put}/\texttt{end}/\texttt{prepare}/\texttt{commit} for a \texttt{txn\_id} before its \texttt{start} has been delivered. An \texttt{abort}, however, may arrive before the \texttt{start} (e.g.\ a global timeout right after the branch was registered). The shim then records a \emph{tombstone} for that \texttt{txn\_id}, so that the late \texttt{start} fails instead of running, voting YES and holding its locks for a global transaction that was rolled back. The shim drops a subtransaction from \texttt{transactions\_map} once it is committed or aborted, and later or duplicate messages for that \texttt{txn\_id} find no entry (\texttt{prepare} votes NO, \texttt{commit} is a no-op, \texttt{get}/\texttt{put}/\texttt{end} fail, \texttt{abort} leaves a tombstone).

\paragraph{Tombstones.}
A tombstone is an entry of \texttt{transactions\_map} with status \texttt{aborted}, no locks and an expiry time. It is kept in the same map as the subtransactions so that \texttt{abort}'s ``known subtransaction, or else tombstone'' and \texttt{start}'s ``no entry, tombstone included'' are each one atomic operation on the same map entry: either the \texttt{start} registers first and the \texttt{abort} finds and aborts the subtransaction, or the \texttt{abort} leaves its tombstone first and the \texttt{start} finds it and fails. There is no window in which the \texttt{abort} misses the subtransaction and the \texttt{start} misses the tombstone; an implementation with a separate tombstone map must preserve this with a per-\texttt{txn\_id} critical section. Every other request on a tombstoned \texttt{txn\_id} needs no new branch, since it finds an aborted subtransaction without locks: \texttt{prepare} votes NO, \texttt{commit} is a no-op, \texttt{get}/\texttt{put}/\texttt{end} fail. A tombstone is only ever created for a \texttt{txn\_id} without an entry, so it never replaces or aborts a registered subtransaction (\texttt{prepared} stays final), and it sits in no chain, so there is nothing to cascade or splice. Tombstones are garbage-collected by the shim after \texttt{tombstone\_ttl}, to be chosen at least as large as the coordinator's maximum global transaction timeout. This is not definitive: if a \texttt{start} arrives after its tombstone expired, the subtransaction is accepted although the global transaction was rolled back; the TTL only makes this unlikely. A tombstone is never removed by a request. Most never meet a \texttt{start} at all: a retried \texttt{abort} of an already evicted subtransaction, or the \texttt{abort} sent after a failed phase 1 to a shim that voted NO, leave one too, since an evicted \texttt{txn\_id} cannot be told from a never-started one. The TTL is what bounds them.

\paragraph{Execution Model.}
The shim never receives or runs client code: a subtransaction reaches it as a sequence of requests over a connection, \texttt{start(txn\_id)}, then one \texttt{get}/\texttt{put} request per operation, then \texttt{end(txn\_id)} once the client sent its last operation, followed by the coordinator's \texttt{prepare} and \texttt{commit} or \texttt{abort}. Each \texttt{get}/\texttt{put} request acquires a lock on its key dynamically, at the point of access, rather than declaring its full read/write set upfront. A subtransaction proceeds speculatively past a conflicting predecessor once that predecessor reaches \texttt{executed} --- its value is fixed even though undecided --- and only blocks on full resolution at \texttt{prepare}, where it waits until every predecessor has committed or has been removed from the chain by an abort. A waiter never aborts merely because it sees an aborted predecessor: the cascade of the aborting transaction dooms exactly its dependents before it is removed, walking the chain behind each of its writes and stopping at the next blind writer, which did not read the aborted value and whose own successors read the blind write instead. A later write to an already-read key triggers a lock upgrade: in place if the subtransaction is the sole reader, otherwise onto a new exclusive node behind the readers. An upgrade may move a subtransaction only across readers, never across a writer, since the value it read would be stale by the time its own write takes effect. If a blind writer (one that never read the key) is already queued behind, the new node goes in front of it; if the node behind is itself an upgrade, i.e.\ its holder read the key too, exactly one of the two read-modify-write subtransactions must abort, and it is the later upgrader. The execution phase of a subtransaction spans its requests from \texttt{start} (status \texttt{started}) to \texttt{end}, which flips the status to \texttt{executed} --- but it is not uninterruptible in the scheduling sense: an individual \texttt{get}/\texttt{put} request can block on \Call{lock}{} waiting for another subtransaction, and a \texttt{FAILED} result from any \texttt{get}/\texttt{put} (a \texttt{MUST\_ABORT} from \Call{lock}{} or from \Call{upgrade}{}'s case 3b) means the subtransaction has already been aborted and its locks released, so the client sends no further operations for it. A cascade or a coordinator abort can also land between two requests or after the last one; \texttt{end} then finds the status no longer \texttt{started}, fails its CAS and calls the idempotent \Call{abort\_transaction}{} itself, since no running request would otherwise release the locks. Requests on an unknown or already evicted \texttt{txn\_id} fail, and a \texttt{get}/\texttt{put} arriving after \texttt{end} is refused without touching the subtransaction. The client may not access the store or acquire locks except through \texttt{get}/\texttt{put} requests. Writes are buffered per-transaction (\texttt{write\_buffer}) and applied to the store only at \texttt{commit}, rather than in place at \texttt{put}-time; the buffer is final once the subtransaction is \texttt{executed}. A \texttt{get} first returns the subtransaction's own buffered write to that key, if any. Otherwise, when it is speculating on a predecessor, it reads the value from that predecessor's \texttt{write\_buffer} rather than from the store: it walks back from its own node to the nearest EXCLUSIVE node (never a successor's) and takes the value from that holder's buffer, which is safe because \Call{lock}{} returns only once every predecessor holder is at least \texttt{executed}. If no such node remains, the writer has already committed and applied its buffer to the store, so the store holds the correct value. A subtransaction votes YES once every predecessor has resolved and its status has been CASed to \texttt{prepared}. On \texttt{commit} the status moves to \texttt{committing}, the buffer is applied to the store and the locks are released; only then is the status \texttt{committed}. The intermediate \texttt{committing} state keeps a coordinator abort from taking a subtransaction whose commit has been decided, and keeps a successor's prepare from passing before the writes are applied.

\paragraph{Architecture.}
There is one shim per data source, where a data source is a node together with a database name. The shim node is colocated with the store or runs standalone next to it. One server can host several databases, each with its own shim, i.e.\ its own \texttt{locks\_map} and \texttt{transactions\_map}; nothing is shared between shims. A shim manages all the tables of its database. Each shim accepts many concurrent connections, only from the coordinator side (the 2PC participants). A connection carries one subtransaction at a time, while many subtransactions run concurrently on different connections; each request runs on the thread handling its connection and blocks only that thread, so the requests of one subtransaction never run concurrently with each other, and the only foreign threads that touch a running subtransaction are cascades and the coordinator's \texttt{abort}/\texttt{prepare}. A subtransaction is bound to a single data source by definition --- access to several databases is expressed as multiple subtransactions within one Sonata global transaction, coordinated by Sonata's outer 2PC, not by any single subtransaction spanning data sources. The shim's \texttt{locks\_map} is therefore scoped to one data source, with a single point of serialization; no cross-node lock coordination is needed within the shim itself.

\paragraph{Data Model.}
The shim locks at the granularity of a single record, identified by its table and its primary key: a key $k$ in the pseudocode stands for the pair (table, key); this is the unit \texttt{get}/\texttt{put} and the \texttt{locks\_map} chains operate on. Values are treated as opaque --- the shim does not interpret or type-check them, only serializes access to them via SHARED/EXCLUSIVE locks.

\begin{algorithmic}[1]

\State \textbf{Data Structures:}
\State $\text{lock\_mode} \in \{\text{SHARED}, \text{EXCLUSIVE}\}$
\State $\text{LockNode}: \{\text{mode}, \text{holders}: \text{set of txn}, \text{predecessor}, \text{next}, \text{upgraded}\}$ \Comment{upgraded (default false): the holder read $k$ before writing it; set by \Call{upgrade}{}}

\State $\text{chain}: \{\text{head}, \text{tail}\}$ \Comment{doubly linked through predecessor/next, arrival order $=$ required commit order; last\_node() $=$ tail}
\State $\text{locks\_map}\colon \texttt{ConcurrentHashMap<key, chain>}$ \Comment{one chain per key, generations of holders; a key $k$ stands for (table, key)}
\State $\text{txn}: \{\text{txn\_id}, \text{locks\_acquired}: \text{key} \to (\text{mode}, \text{target\_node}) \text{ (built dynamically)}, \text{status}, \text{write\_buffer}: \text{key} \to \text{value}, \text{expires\_at}\}$ \Comment{expires\_at: only set on a tombstone, null otherwise}
\State $\text{status} \in \{\text{started}, \text{executed}, \text{prepared}, \text{committing}, \text{committed}, \text{aborted}, \text{must\_abort}\}$
\State $\text{transactions\_map}\colon \texttt{ConcurrentHashMap<txn\_id, txn>}$ \Comment{also holds the tombstones: a txn entry with status aborted, no locks and expires\_at set, recording that the coordinator aborted the id before its start; same map so that \Call{abort}{} and \Call{start}{} are each one atomic operation on the entry and other requests need no new branch}
\State $\text{tombstone\_ttl}$: duration \Comment{lifetime of a tombstone, $\geq$ the coordinator's maximum global transaction timeout}
\State $\text{lock\_timeout}$: duration \Comment{bounds every wait of the execution phase (\Call{lock}{}, \Call{upgrade}{}); prepare has none}
\State \Comment{atomic(locks\_map) is one critical section; per-key latches are fine if the status re-check and the update of locks\_acquired stay atomic w.r.t. \Call{abort\_transaction}{} reading locks\_acquired}

\Statex
\State \textbf{Store:} black box NoSQL store, nothing more is assumed than the following
\State \Call{store.read}{k} $\to$ value
\State \Call{store.write}{k, v} \Comment{blind put; no atomicity across keys assumed}
\State \Comment{store.write is blocking and retried until it succeeds; crash recovery is out of scope (the shim is assumed not to crash between voting YES and the decision)}

\Statex
\State \textbf{Requests:} \Comment{the shim never receives or runs client code}
\State \Comment{one shim per data source ($=$ node $+$ database name), colocated with the store or standalone; several databases on one server each have their own shim (own locks\_map / transactions\_map); a shim manages all the tables of its database}
\State \Comment{each shim accepts many concurrent connections, only from the coordinator side (the 2PC participants); a connection carries one txn at a time: \Call{start}{txn\_id}, one \Call{get}{}/\Call{put}{} per operation, then \Call{end}{txn\_id}; prepare/commit/abort carry the coordinator's decision}
\State \Comment{each request runs on the thread handling its connection, so the requests of one txn never run concurrently; only cascades and the coordinator's abort/prepare touch a running txn from another thread}

\Statex
\Procedure{start}{txn\_id}
    \State $txn \gets$ new txn with $\text{txn\_id}$, $\text{locks\_acquired}=\{\}$, $\text{status}=\text{started}$, $\text{write\_buffer}=\{\}$
    \If{$\Call{transactions\_map.putIfAbsent}{txn.\text{txn\_id}, txn} \neq \text{null}$}
        \State \textbf{return} FAILED \Comment{txn\_id already registered (collision / duplicate) or tombstoned: the coordinator already aborted it. putIfAbsent is atomic with the computeIfAbsent of \Call{abort}{}: no window where the abort misses the txn and the start misses the tombstone}
    \EndIf
    \State \textbf{return} SUCCEEDED
\EndProcedure

\Statex
\Procedure{end}{txn\_id} \Comment{the client sent its last get/put: the write buffer is final from here on}
    \State $txn \gets \text{transactions\_map}[txn\_id]$
    \If{$txn == \text{null}$}
        \State \textbf{return} FAILED \Comment{unknown txn\_id: never started, or already aborted and evicted; a tombstoned id fails the CAS below instead}
    \EndIf
    \If{\textbf{not} \Call{CAS}{txn.\text{status}, \text{started}, \text{executed}}}
        \If{$txn.\text{status} \in \{\text{executed}, \text{prepared}, \text{committing}, \text{committed}\}$}
            \State \textbf{return} SUCCEEDED \Comment{duplicate/late end: never abort here, a prepared txn voted YES}
        \EndIf
        \State \Comment{aborted or must\_abort: a FAILED get/put already aborted it, but a cascade or foreign abort may have landed after the last get/put and nobody released the locks yet}
        \State \Call{abort\_transaction}{txn} \Comment{idempotent}
        \State \textbf{return} FAILED
    \EndIf
    \State \textbf{return} SUCCEEDED
\EndProcedure

\Statex
\Procedure{get}{txn\_id, k}
    \State $txn \gets \text{transactions\_map}[txn\_id]$
    \If{$txn == \text{null}$}
        \State \textbf{return NULL, FAILED} \Comment{unknown txn\_id: never started, or already aborted and evicted; a tombstoned id fails in \Call{lock}{} before touching any chain}
    \EndIf
    \If{$txn.\text{status} \in \{\text{executed}, \text{prepared}, \text{committing}, \text{committed}\}$}
        \State \textbf{return NULL, FAILED} \Comment{request after \Call{end}{}: the buffer is final, refuse without aborting; the only FAILED not coming from \Call{abort\_transaction}{}}
    \EndIf
    \State $result \gets$ \Call{lock}{k, \text{SHARED}, txn}
    \If{$result == \text{MUST\_ABORT}$}
        \State \Call{abort\_transaction}{txn}
        \State \textbf{return NULL, FAILED}
    \EndIf
    \If{$k \in txn.\text{write\_buffer}$}
        \State \textbf{return} $txn.\text{write\_buffer}[k]$, SUCCEEDED \Comment{own write first}
    \EndIf
    \State $actual\_value \gets \text{null}$ \Comment{null $=$ no predecessor version; values are assumed non-null}
    \Statex \Comment{speculative read: nearest EXCLUSIVE node at or before own node; its buffer is final since \Call{lock}{} waits for \texttt{executed}. If none, the writer committed and the store is correct}
    \State \textbf{atomic}(locks\_map) \{
    \If{$k \in \text{locks\_map}$}
        \State $current\_node \gets txn.\text{locks\_acquired}[k].\text{target\_node}$
        \While{$current\_node \neq \text{null}$ \textbf{and} $current\_node.\text{mode} \neq \text{EXCLUSIVE}$}
            \State $current\_node \gets current\_node.\text{predecessor}$
        \EndWhile
        \If{$current\_node \neq \text{null}$}
            \State $actual\_value \gets (\text{sole holder of } current\_node).\text{write\_buffer}[k]$
        \EndIf
    \EndIf
    \}
    \If{$actual\_value \neq \text{null}$}
        \State \textbf{return} $actual\_value$, SUCCEEDED
    \EndIf
    \State \textbf{return} \Call{store.read}{k}, SUCCEEDED
\EndProcedure

\Statex
\Procedure{put}{txn\_id, k, v}
    \State $txn \gets \text{transactions\_map}[txn\_id]$
    \If{$txn == \text{null}$}
        \State \textbf{return FAILED} \Comment{unknown txn\_id, as in \Call{get}{}}
    \EndIf
    \If{$txn.\text{status} \in \{\text{executed}, \text{prepared}, \text{committing}, \text{committed}\}$}
        \State \textbf{return FAILED} \Comment{request after \Call{end}{}, as in \Call{get}{}}
    \EndIf
    \State $result \gets$ \Call{lock}{k, \text{EXCLUSIVE}, txn}
    \If{$result == \text{MUST\_ABORT}$}
        \State \Call{abort\_transaction}{txn}
        \State \textbf{return FAILED}
    \EndIf
    \State $txn.\text{write\_buffer}[k] \gets v$
    \State \textbf{return SUCCEEDED}
\EndProcedure

\end{algorithmic}

\Statex
\Procedure{lock}{k, mode, txn}
    \If{$\text{txn.status} == \text{aborted}$ \textbf{or} $\text{txn.status} == \text{must\_abort}$}
        \State \textbf{return} MUST\_ABORT \Comment{txn was cascade-aborted while running}
    \EndIf
    \State $target\_node \gets \text{null}$
    \If{$\text{txn.locks\_acquired}[k]$ contains $(mode, *)$}
        \State \textbf{return} ALREADY\_HELD
    \ElsIf{$\text{txn.locks\_acquired}[k]$ contains $(\text{EXCLUSIVE}, *)$}
        \State \textbf{return} ALREADY\_HELD
    \ElsIf{$\text{txn.locks\_acquired}[k]$ contains $(\text{SHARED}, *)$ \textbf{and} $mode == \text{EXCLUSIVE}$}
        \State \textbf{return} \Call{upgrade}{k, txn}
    \EndIf
    \State \textbf{atomic}(locks\_map) \{
        \If{$\text{txn.status} \in \{\text{aborted}, \text{must\_abort}\}$}
            \State \indent \textbf{return} MUST\_ABORT \Comment{re-check under the latch: a foreign \Call{abort\_transaction}{} may have released our locks after the check above}
        \EndIf
        \If{$k \notin \text{locks\_map}$}
            \State \indent $\text{locks\_map}[k] = \text{new chain}$
        \EndIf
        \State \indent $chain \gets \text{locks\_map}[k]$
        \State \indent $last\_node \gets chain.\text{last\_node}()$
        \If{$last\_node == \text{null}$}
            \State \indent $target\_node \gets \text{LockNode}\{mode, \text{holders}=\{txn\}, \text{predecessor}=\text{null}, \text{next}=\text{null}\}$
            \State \indent append $target\_node$ to $chain$
        \ElsIf{$mode == \text{SHARED}$ \textbf{and} $last\_node.\text{mode} == \text{SHARED}$}
            \State \indent $last\_node.\text{holders.add}(txn)$
            \State \indent $target\_node \gets last\_node$
        \Else
            \State \indent $target\_node \gets \text{LockNode}\{mode, \text{holders}=\{txn\}, \text{predecessor}=last\_node, \text{next}=\text{null}\}$
            \State \indent $last\_node.\text{next} \gets target\_node$
            \State \indent append $target\_node$ to $chain$
        \EndIf
        \State \indent $\text{txn.locks\_acquired}[k] = (mode, target\_node)$
    \}
    \State $deadline \gets \text{now}() + \text{lock\_timeout}$
    \Loop
        \State \textbf{atomic}(locks\_map) \{ $pred \gets target\_node.\text{predecessor}$ \}
        \If{$pred == \text{null}$}
            \State \textbf{break}
        \EndIf
        \State \Comment{pred may be spliced out while waiting: re-read it under the latch each iteration}
        \State \textbf{wait until} $\big(\forall t \in pred.\text{holders}, t.\text{status} \in \{\text{committed}, \text{executed}, \text{prepared}, \text{committing}\}\big)$ \textbf{or} $\text{txn.status} \in \{\text{aborted}, \text{must\_abort}\}$ \textbf{or} $pred \neq target\_node.\text{predecessor}$ \textbf{or} $\text{now}() \geq deadline$
        \If{$pred \neq target\_node.\text{predecessor}$}
            \State \textbf{continue} \Comment{pred spliced out mid-wait, re-read}
        \EndIf
        \State \Comment{no abort on an aborted/doomed predecessor holder: it does not satisfy the wait, so this txn waits for its splice; whether it depends on it is decided by the cascade, which marks exactly the dependents must\_abort before the splice}
        \If{$\text{txn.status} \in \{\text{aborted}, \text{must\_abort}\}$}
            \State \textbf{return} MUST\_ABORT
        \EndIf
        \If{\textbf{not} \big(\forall t \in pred.\text{holders}, t.\text{status} \in \{\text{committed}, \text{executed}, \text{prepared}, \text{committing}\}\big)}
            \State \Call{abort\_transaction}{txn} \Comment{deadline reached: assume a deadlock, safe since nothing was voted}
            \State \textbf{return} MUST\_ABORT
        \EndIf
        \State \textbf{break}
    \EndLoop
    \State \Comment{deadlocks between running txns are broken by lock\_timeout, no wait-for graph is kept}
    \State \textbf{return} LOCK\_ACQUIRED
\EndProcedure

\Statex
\Procedure{upgrade}{k, txn}
    \State $must\_wait \gets \text{false}$
    \State $node \gets \text{null}$
    \State $return\_value \gets \text{MUST\_ABORT}$
    \State \textbf{atomic}(locks\_map) \{
        \If{$\text{txn.status} \in \{\text{aborted}, \text{must\_abort}\}$}
            \State \indent \textbf{return} MUST\_ABORT \Comment{re-check under the latch, as in \Call{lock}{}}
        \EndIf
        \State \indent $node \gets \text{txn.locks\_acquired}[k].\text{target\_node}$
        \State \indent $chain \gets \text{locks\_map}[k]$
        \State \indent \Comment{an upgrade may move this txn only across readers, never across a writer; what is queued behind node is blocked in \Call{lock}{} until node's holders (this txn included) are executed}
        \State \indent $p \gets node$
        \While{$p.\text{next} \neq \text{null}$ \textbf{and} $p.\text{next}.\text{mode} == \text{SHARED}$}
            \State \indent $p \gets p.\text{next}$ \Comment{readers left by a spliced-out aborted writer}
        \EndWhile
        \State \indent $after \gets p.\text{next}$ \Comment{EXCLUSIVE or null}
        \If{$after \neq \text{null}$ \textbf{and} $after.\text{upgraded}$}
            \State \indent \Comment{case 3b: its holder read $k$ too (read-modify-write vs read-modify-write): this txn loses, return\_value stays MUST\_ABORT}
        \ElsIf{$p == node$ \textbf{and} $node.\text{holders} == \{txn\}$}
            \State \indent $node.\text{mode} \gets \text{EXCLUSIVE}$; $node.\text{upgraded} \gets \text{true}$ \Comment{case 1: in place, at most a blind writer behind}
            \State \indent $\text{txn.locks\_acquired}[k] = (\text{EXCLUSIVE}, node)$
            \State \indent $return\_value \gets \text{UPGRADED}$
        \Else
            \State \indent $node.\text{holders.remove}(txn)$ \Comment{cases 2, 3a: new node right behind $p$, at the tail or in front of the blind writer $after$}
            \State \indent $new\_node \gets \text{LockNode}\{\text{EXCLUSIVE}, \text{holders}=\{txn\}, \text{predecessor}=p, \text{next}=after, \text{upgraded}=\text{true}\}$
            \State \indent $p.\text{next} \gets new\_node$
            \If{$after \neq \text{null}$}
                \State \indent $after.\text{predecessor} \gets new\_node$ \Comment{it now also waits for this txn}
            \Else
                \State \indent append $new\_node$ to $chain$
            \EndIf
            \If{$node.\text{holders}$ is empty} \Comment{only possible when $p \neq node$}
                \State \indent \textbf{if} $node.\text{predecessor} \neq \text{null}$\textbf{:} $node.\text{predecessor.next} \gets node.\text{next}$
                \State \indent \textbf{if} $node.\text{next} \neq \text{null}$\textbf{:} $node.\text{next.predecessor} \gets node.\text{predecessor}$
                \State \indent remove $node$ from $chain$
            \EndIf
            \State \indent $\text{txn.locks\_acquired}[k] = (\text{EXCLUSIVE}, new\_node)$
            \State \indent $must\_wait \gets \text{true}$
            \State \indent $return\_value \gets \text{UPGRADED}$
        \EndIf
    \}
    \If{$must\_wait$}
        \State $deadline \gets \text{now}() + \text{lock\_timeout}$
        \Loop
            \State \textbf{atomic}(locks\_map) \{ $pred \gets new\_node.\text{predecessor}$ \}
            \If{$pred == \text{null}$}
                \State \textbf{break}
            \EndIf
            \State \Comment{same re-read of the predecessor as in \Call{lock}{}}
            \State \textbf{wait until} $\big(\forall t \in pred.\text{holders}, t.\text{status} \in \{\text{committed}, \text{executed}, \text{prepared}, \text{committing}\}\big)$ \textbf{or} $\text{txn.status} \in \{\text{aborted}, \text{must\_abort}\}$ \textbf{or} $pred \neq new\_node.\text{predecessor}$ \textbf{or} $\text{now}() \geq deadline$
            \If{$pred \neq new\_node.\text{predecessor}$}
                \State \textbf{continue}
            \EndIf
            \State \Comment{no abort on an aborted/doomed predecessor holder, as in \Call{lock}{}: wait for its splice}
            \If{$\text{txn.status} \in \{\text{aborted}, \text{must\_abort}\}$}
                \State \textbf{return} MUST\_ABORT
            \EndIf
            \If{\textbf{not} \big(\forall t \in pred.\text{holders}, t.\text{status} \in \{\text{committed}, \text{executed}, \text{prepared}, \text{committing}\}\big)}
                \State \Call{abort\_transaction}{txn} \Comment{deadline reached, as in \Call{lock}{}}
                \State \textbf{return} MUST\_ABORT
            \EndIf
            \State \textbf{break}
        \EndLoop
        \State \Comment{bounded by lock\_timeout, like the wait in \Call{lock}{}}
    \EndIf
    \State \textbf{return} $return\_value$
\EndProcedure

\end{algorithmic}

\begin{algorithmic}[1]

\Procedure{prepare}{txn\_id}
    \State $txn \gets \text{transactions\_map}[txn\_id]$
    \If{$txn == \text{null}$}
        \State \Comment{unknown txn\_id: late/duplicate prepare of a resolved txn}
        \State \textbf{Send no to 2PC controller}
        \State \textbf{return}
    \EndIf
    \If{$txn.\text{status} \in \{\text{prepared}, \text{committing}, \text{committed}\}$}
        \State \Comment{duplicate/late prepare: the txn already voted YES, re-send it}
        \State \textbf{Send yes to 2PC controller}
        \State \textbf{return}
    \EndIf
    \If{$txn.\text{status} \notin \{\text{executed}, \text{prepared}\}$}
        \State \Comment{covers aborted and must\_abort, and a tombstoned id (status aborted)}
        \State \textbf{Send no to 2PC controller}
        \State \Call{abort\_transaction}{txn}
        \State \textbf{return}
    \EndIf

    \For{\textbf{each} $(k, mode, node) \in \text{txn.locks\_acquired}$}
        \Loop
            \State \textbf{atomic}(locks\_map) \{ $pred \gets node.\text{predecessor}$ \}
            \If{$pred == \text{null}$}
                \State \textbf{break}
            \EndIf
            \State \Comment{an aborted/doomed holder does not satisfy this wait: wait until it is spliced out. If this txn depended on it, the cascade already marked it must\_abort before the splice (caught by the status check below); if not (a blind writer behind an aborted writer, anyone behind an aborted reader) it goes on and still waits for whatever is now ahead of it}
            \State \textbf{wait until} $\big(\forall t \in pred.\text{holders}, t.\text{status} == \text{committed}\big)$ \textbf{or} $\text{txn.status} \in \{\text{aborted}, \text{must\_abort}\}$ \textbf{or} $pred \neq node.\text{predecessor}$
            \State \Comment{may have been cascade-aborted via another key}
            \If{$\text{txn.status} \in \{\text{aborted}, \text{must\_abort}\}$}
                \State \textbf{Send no to 2PC controller}
                \If{$\text{txn.status} == \text{must\_abort}$}
                    \State \Call{abort\_transaction}{txn}
                \EndIf
                \State \textbf{return}
            \EndIf
            \If{$pred \neq node.\text{predecessor}$}
                \State \textbf{continue}
            \EndIf
            \State \textbf{break}
        \EndLoop
    \EndFor

    \If{\textbf{not} \Call{CAS}{\text{txn.status}, \{executed, prepared\}, prepared}}
        \If{$txn.\text{status} \in \{\text{prepared}, \text{committing}, \text{committed}\}$}
            \State \Comment{a concurrent duplicate prepare already voted YES: voting NO now would contradict it, re-send YES}
            \State \textbf{Send yes to 2PC controller}
            \State \textbf{return}
        \EndIf
        \State \Comment{late cascade mark: the txn never prepared, so abort it and release its locks}
        \State \textbf{Send no to 2PC controller}
        \State \Call{abort\_transaction}{txn}
        \State \textbf{return}
    \EndIf
    \State \textbf{Send yes to 2PC controller}
\EndProcedure

\Statex
\Procedure{commit}{txn\_id}
    \State $txn \gets \text{transactions\_map}[txn\_id]$
    \If{$txn == \text{null}$}
        \State \textbf{return} \Comment{unknown/already-evicted txn\_id: no-op}
    \EndIf
    \If{\textbf{not} \Call{CAS}{\text{txn.status}, \text{prepared}, \text{committing}}}
        \State \textbf{return} \Comment{includes a tombstoned id: no-op}
    \EndIf
    \State \Comment{committing, not committed, while the store is written: a coordinator abort can no longer take the txn, and a successor's prepare cannot pass before the writes are applied}
    \For{\textbf{each} $(key, value) \in txn.\text{write\_buffer}$}
        \State $\text{store.write}(key, value)$
    \EndFor
    \State \textbf{atomic}(locks\_map) \{
        \For{\textbf{each} $(k, mode, target\_node) \in \text{txn.locks\_acquired}$}
            \State \indent $chain \gets \text{locks\_map}[k]$
            \State \indent remove $txn$ from $target\_node.\text{holders}$
            \If{$target\_node.\text{holders}$ is empty}
                \State \indent\indent \textbf{if} $target\_node.\text{predecessor} \neq \text{null}$\textbf{:} $target\_node.\text{predecessor.next} \gets target\_node.\text{next}$
                \State \indent\indent \textbf{if} $target\_node.\text{next} \neq \text{null}$\textbf{:} $target\_node.\text{next.predecessor} \gets target\_node.\text{predecessor}$
                \State \indent\indent remove $target\_node$ from $chain$
                \State \indent\indent \textbf{if} $chain$ is empty\textbf{:} remove $k$ from $\text{locks\_map}$ \Comment{under the latch}
            \EndIf
        \EndFor
    \}
    \State $\text{txn.status} \gets \text{committed}$
    \State \Call{transactions\_map.remove}{txn.\text{txn\_id}} \Comment{resolved: volatile state dropped; the coordinator owns the txn\_id lifecycle}
\EndProcedure

\Statex
\Procedure{abort\_transaction}{txn}
    \If{\textbf{not} \Call{CAS}{\text{txn.status}, \{\text{executed}, \text{prepared}, \text{must\_abort}, \text{started}\}, \text{aborted}}}
        \State \textbf{return}
    \EndIf

    \State $doomed\_finished \gets \{\}$ \Comment{cascade victims (txn objects) that had already finished executing}
    \State \textbf{atomic}(locks\_map) \{
        \For{\textbf{each} $(k, mode, target\_node) \in \text{txn.locks\_acquired}$}
            \State \indent $chain \gets \text{locks\_map}[k]$
            \If{$mode == \text{EXCLUSIVE}$}
                \State \indent\indent $successor\_node \gets target\_node.\text{next}$
                \While{$successor\_node \neq \text{null}$}
                    \If{$successor\_node.\text{mode} == \text{EXCLUSIVE}$ \textbf{and not} $successor\_node.\text{upgraded}$}
                        \State \indent\indent\indent\indent \textbf{break} \Comment{blind write}
                    \EndIf
                    \State \indent\indent\indent \textbf{for each} $t \in successor\_node.\text{holders}$\textbf{:} \textbf{if} \Call{mark\_must\_abort}{t}\textbf{:} $doomed\_finished\text{.add}(t)$
                    \State \indent\indent\indent $successor\_node \gets successor\_node.\text{next}$
                \EndWhile
            \EndIf
            \State \indent remove $txn$ from $target\_node.\text{holders}$
            \If{$target\_node.\text{holders}$ is empty}
                \State \indent\indent \textbf{if} $target\_node.\text{predecessor} \neq \text{null}$\textbf{:} $target\_node.\text{predecessor.next} \gets target\_node.\text{next}$
                \State \indent\indent \textbf{if} $target\_node.\text{next} \neq \text{null}$\textbf{:} $target\_node.\text{next.predecessor} \gets target\_node.\text{predecessor}$
                \State \indent\indent remove $target\_node$ from $chain$
                \State \indent\indent \textbf{if} $chain$ is empty\textbf{:} remove $k$ from $\text{locks\_map}$
            \EndIf
        \EndFor
    \}
    \State \Call{transactions\_map.remove}{txn.\text{txn\_id}} \Comment{resolved: evicted like in \Call{commit}{}; successors hold a reference and observe txn.status directly, a late abort for the id releases nothing, it only leaves a tombstone}

    \State \textbf{for each} $t \in doomed\_finished$\textbf{:} \Call{abort\_transaction}{t} \Comment{eager cleanup, outside the latch: a doomed txn already \texttt{executed} has no thread left to abort it; safe even if $t$ was already resolved elsewhere, since its leading CAS is an idempotent no-op on a status that no longer matches}
\EndProcedure

\Statex
\Procedure{abort}{txn\_id}
    \State \Comment{unknown id: leave a tombstone, since the abort may precede the start; otherwise the late start would run, vote YES and hold its locks although the global transaction was rolled back}
    \State $txn \gets$ \Call{transactions\_map.computeIfAbsent}{txn\_id, tombstone} \Comment{tombstone $=$ new txn with txn\_id, locks\_acquired$=\{\}$, status$=$aborted, write\_buffer$=\{\}$, expires\_at$=$now()$+$tombstone\_ttl}
    \If{$txn$ is the tombstone just inserted}
        \State \textbf{return} \Comment{atomic with the putIfAbsent of \Call{start}{}}
    \EndIf
    \State \Call{abort\_transaction}{txn} \Comment{a registered txn (unchanged), or an older tombstone: a no-op, its CAS fails on aborted, expires\_at is not refreshed}
    \State \Comment{a tombstone is never removed by a request, only by \Call{gc\_tombstones}{}; most never meet a start (retried abort of an evicted txn, abort after a failed phase 1): tombstone\_ttl bounds them. It is only created for an id with no entry and holds no lock: prepared stays final, nothing to cascade or splice, no node can leak}
\EndProcedure

\Statex
\Procedure{gc\_tombstones}{} \Comment{runs periodically, in the background}
    \For{\textbf{each} $(txn\_id, t) \in \text{transactions\_map}$}
        \If{$t.\text{expires\_at} \neq \text{null}$ \textbf{and} $\text{now}() \geq t.\text{expires\_at}$}
            \State \Call{transactions\_map.remove}{txn\_id, t} \Comment{only if the entry is still this tombstone}
        \EndIf
    \EndFor
    \State \Comment{not definitive: a start arriving after its tombstone expired is accepted although the global transaction was rolled back; tombstone\_ttl only makes this unlikely}
\EndProcedure

\Statex
\Procedure{mark\_must\_abort}{txn} \Comment{returns true iff txn was \texttt{executed}: the caller must clean it up itself. Takes the txn object directly, like \Call{abort\_transaction}{}: only ever called from a cascade already holding a live reference to the victim, never from the coordinator}
    \State \Comment{a started victim is still running: the thread handling its connection aborts it at its next get/put (\Call{lock}{}/\Call{upgrade}{} see must\_abort) or at the CAS in \Call{end}{}}
    \State $before \gets \Call{CAS\_returning}{\text{txn.status}, \{\text{executed}, \text{started}\}, \text{must\_abort}}$
    \State \textbf{return} $before == \text{executed}$
\EndProcedure

\end{algorithmic}
